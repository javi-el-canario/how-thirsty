# AI pictures: prompts for Gemini (001-060)

60 scenes, 18 kinds of photo (casual phone snapshots, flash, old cameras, night, security cameras... and also professional ones: DSLR, golden hour, long exposure, drone, macro, film, studio). Paste the **full prompt** into Gemini (text only).

Every prompt ends with a rule that keeps the photographer out of the picture (no hands, feet, shadow or phone in view). **Words to avoid** in your own prompts for the casual ones: beautiful, stunning, epic, cinematic, professional, bokeh, HDR, 4K.

**How to save:** name the files `01.png`, `02.png` ... `60.png` (same order as this list) in your own folder, then run
`python tools/prepare-images.py "C:/path/to/your/ai-folder" --kind ai` and `node tools/build-pictures-json.mjs`.
(They are numbered in file-name order, so keep the order. If you work in batches, run the script once per batch, in order.)

**Tries:** how many times Gemini computed the image until you kept it (first result = 1, every 'generate again' or edit +1). Tell me the real numbers if they are not 1.
